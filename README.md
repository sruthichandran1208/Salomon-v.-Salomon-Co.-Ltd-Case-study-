# Salomon-v.-Salomon-Co.-Ltd-Case-study-

### Salomon v. Salomon & Co. Ltd – Case Study 

A case study on **Salomon v. Salomon & Co. Ltd**, an important case in company law. It explains the facts of the case, the main issue, the court’s decision, and the principle of separate legal personality. This case helped me understand that a company has its own legal identity separate from its members.
